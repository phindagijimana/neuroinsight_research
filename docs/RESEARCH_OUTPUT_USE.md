# Research use of outputs

## Policy

**All outputs** produced by NeuroInsight workflows and plugins — including URMC
Radiology and Neurology workflows, TRACK-TBI, and any other use case — are
intended for **research use only** as delivered by this platform.

NeuroInsight **does not** provide medical advice, diagnoses, or treatment
recommendations, and **does not** represent outputs as FDA-cleared or
clinically validated for any specific use.

### Clinical support (user responsibility only)

Outputs **may** be used by the **user** as **clinical support** (e.g. quantitative
adjuncts under read workflows) **only if the user and their institution** choose to
do so, obtain all required **IRB**, contractual, and regulatory approvals, perform
any **validation** their policies require, and comply with **tool licenses**
(FreeSurfer, MELD, etc.) and professional standards.

That choice and any consequences are **solely the user’s responsibility** — not
NeuroInsight’s.

## Summary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Default intended use of outputs** | **Research only** |
| **Clinical support** | **Permitted only at user/institution responsibility** with appropriate governance |
| **Platform role** | Orchestrate pipelines; deliver files (images, tables, reports, logs) |
| **Not provided** | Diagnostic claims, clearance, or guarantee of fitness for a particular clinical use |
